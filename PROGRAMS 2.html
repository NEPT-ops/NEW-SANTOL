Barangay Santol

Quezon City, Metro Manila, Philippines

Barangay Santol is a community where neighbors know each other and work together to keep the area safe and peaceful.
It’s a great neighborhood filled with friendly people, helpful leaders, and a strong sense of unity. In this barangay, everyone contributes
to making the place clean, organized, and welcoming for families and visitors. It’s a community
where cooperation and care make daily life better for everyone.
